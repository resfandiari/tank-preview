About War Tanks

War Tanks — City Crusher is a free 3D tank game you play in your browser. Start small, grow by collecting ammo, ram smaller tanks and end up flattening the city.

Play War Tanks

What kind of game is it?

War Tanks is a fast arcade "grow" game. Each match drops you into a city full of rival tanks. You get bigger by picking up ammo crates and by crushing tanks smaller than you. The bigger you get, the more of the city you can destroy: first cars, then houses, then almost everything. One mistake against a bigger tank and your run is over. Rounds are short, so it is easy to play one more.

What makes it different

  • Mass is everything. Your mass is your size, your health and your ammo. Every shot costs mass, so each one is a decision.
  • A city you can crush. Cars and houses are physical objects. As you move up tiers you smash through them instead of driving around them.
  • Automatic aiming. The turret picks the nearest enemy, so you can focus on driving and timing. That makes the game easy to pick up on a phone.
  • City events. Supply drops, golden meteors and police helicopters keep each match different.
  • Daily, weekly and all-time leaderboards ranked by peak mass.

Where can I play?

Anywhere with a modern browser: Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari, on desktop, laptop, phone or tablet. There is nothing to download and no account to create. On a phone you can add War Tanks to your home screen. It then opens full screen in landscape and works offline after the first load.

Is it free?

Yes. The whole game is free. Every skin is cosmetic and either free or unlocked by playing; nothing is for sale. Ads on the site help pay for hosting and development.

Is it suitable for kids?

War Tanks is cartoon-style vehicle combat. The tanks, cars and buildings are colourful toy-like shapes and there is no blood. There is no chat between players. Leaderboard names pass a profanity filter.

Who makes it?

War Tanks is made by a small independent developer. The game is built with modern web technology (TypeScript and WebGL via Three.js), and every tank, car and building is generated in code. We keep improving it based on player feedback, so if you have an idea or found a bug, please get in touch.

Frequently asked questions

Why did I lose mass when I fired?

Shooting costs mass by design. Hits give most of it back; misses do not. See How to play for the full rules.

Why can't I ram a tank that looks about my size?

Ramming only destroys tanks with at least 20% less mass than yours.

The game is slow on my phone. What can I do?

Open Settings and set Graphics quality to Low. That turns off shadows and glow effects. With Auto, the game lowers the quality by itself when the frame rate drops.

Does my progress save?

Your name, skins, settings and personal best are saved in your browser. Clearing your browser data resets them. See the privacy policy for details.
