How to play War Tanks

Eat ammo. Crush the city. Don't get crushed. This guide covers the controls, the rules that decide who wins a fight, and the tactics that get you to the top of the leaderboard.

Play now — it's free

The goal

You drive a small tank through a busy city full of rival tanks. Everything you do is about mass. Mass is your size, your strength and your health all at once. Grow as big as you can before someone bigger crushes you. Your best run is measured by its peak mass, and that is the number the leaderboard ranks.

Controls

Keyboard and mouse

W A S D Drive
Mouse click Fire a shell (the turret aims for you)
Shift (hold) Turbo boost
G Throw a grenade
M Mute or unmute
Esc Back to the menu (asks first)

Phone and tablet

Drag anywhere on the left half of the screen to drive with a virtual joystick. Tap FIRE to shoot, hold TURBO to boost and tap GRENADE to throw. You can change the joystick size and dead zone in Settings. Turn your phone sideways: the game plays best in landscape.

The three rules

1. Collect ammo to grow

Ammo crates are scattered across the streets. Drive over them to gain mass. A bigger tank is stronger but slower and easier to spot, so growing is always a trade.

2. Ram smaller tanks

Drive into a tank that has at least 20% less mass than yours and it is destroyed instantly. You take its mass. If the other tank is close to your size, ramming does not work, so check sizes before you charge. The same rule protects you: stay away from anything clearly bigger.

3. Shooting costs mass

Every shell you fire is paid for with your own mass. A shell that hits a tank or a helicopter gives most of that cost back, but a miss is mass thrown away. Shoot when you are likely to hit, and save your shells for tanks bigger than you, which you cannot ram.

Growing through tiers

As your mass grows you move up tiers, and the city starts to give way:

  • Tier 1: you are small. Collect ammo and avoid big tanks.
  • Tier 2: you can crush cars by driving over them.
  • Tier 3: houses are no longer walls. Drive straight through them.
  • Tier 4: the city is yours. Almost nothing stops you.

The HUD shows how much more mass you need for the next tier.

Turbo and grenades

Turbo gives you a burst of speed. A full gauge lasts about 2.5 seconds and drains only while you hold the button, then refills in about 7 seconds after you let go. Use it to catch a fleeing tank or to escape one that is chasing you.

Grenades cost mass and have a 6-second cooldown. They fly in an arc of about 40 metres, over buildings, and explode in a wide blast. They are the best way to hit a tank hiding behind cover.

Events in the city

  • Supply drops fall from the sky. Claim one for free shots that cost no mass.
  • Golden meteors are announced before they land. Be at the impact site to claim a big mass bonus, but expect every other tank to have the same idea.
  • Police helicopters chase large tanks that cause a lot of destruction. They fly over buildings, so hiding in an alley does not help. Shoot them down or keep moving.
  • No camping. If you park in one spot for too long, nearby tanks start hunting you and a police patrol is sent after you. Keep moving.

Tips for a high score

  1. Early on, ignore fights and collect every crate you can see.
  2. Before ramming, compare sizes. The 20% rule decides the fight, not speed.
  3. Don't spray shells. Each miss shrinks you and makes you a target.
  4. Keep turbo for escapes, not for cruising.
  5. Use grenades against tanks behind buildings.
  6. Watch the radar for big tanks coming your way and leave before they arrive.
  7. When you reach Tier 3, cut through houses to take shortcuts other tanks cannot.

Skins

Skins are cosmetic only and never change how your tank plays. Most are free from the start; the Champion skin unlocks when you reach Tier 4 and the Wrecker skin after you crush 100 cars.
