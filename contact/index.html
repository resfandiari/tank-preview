Reporting a bug

To help us fix it quickly, please include:

  • your device and browser (for example, "iPhone 15, Safari" or "Windows, Chrome"),
  • what you were doing when it happened,
  • what you expected and what happened instead,
  • a screenshot or short screen recording, if you can.

Leaderboard and privacy requests

To remove a leaderboard entry or ask about your data, send the tank name and the approximate date of the run. See our privacy policy for what we store.
