Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

This policy explains what information War Tanks ("we", "the game") collects when you play at wartanks.fun, how it is used, and the choices you have. War Tanks is a free browser game. You do not need an account to play.

Information stored on your device

The game saves a few things in your browser's local storage so they are still there next time:

  • the tank name you type on the start screen,
  • your selected skin and which skins you have unlocked,
  • your settings (volume, graphics quality, screen shake, touch-control size),
  • your personal best and simple progress counters (for example, cars crushed).

This data stays in your browser. We cannot read it unless it is part of a leaderboard entry (see below). You can delete it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

If you install War Tanks as an app, a service worker caches the game's files on your device so it loads faster and works offline. It does not track you.

Leaderboard

When a run ends, the game sends a leaderboard entry to our server: the tank name you chose, your skin, and the run's statistics (peak mass, tanks crushed, objects crushed and survival time). These entries are public and appear in the in-game Ranks panel. Do not use your real name or any personal information as your tank name. Names pass a profanity filter, and we may hide any entry.

Server logs

The site is hosted on Vercel. Like most websites, the hosting provider automatically records technical request data such as IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time of the request. This is used to run, secure and debug the service and is kept only for a limited time. See Vercel's privacy policy.

Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense to show ads, which keeps the game free. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info.
  • Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you are asked for consent before personalized ads or advertising cookies are used, and you can change your choice at any time. If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalized ads.

Children

War Tanks is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted personal information as a leaderboard name, contact us and we will remove it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data about you, or to object to how it is processed. Because we do not run accounts, the only data we hold on our server is leaderboard entries and short-lived server logs. To ask us to remove a leaderboard entry, contact us with the tank name and the approximate date of the run.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.

Contact

Questions about this policy: hello@wartanks.fun, or see the contact page.
