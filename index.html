How to play

Everything in War Tanks is about mass: it is your size, your strength and your health. Drive over ammo crates to grow. Ram any tank with at least 20% less mass than yours to destroy it and take its mass. Shooting costs some of your own mass, so save your shells for tanks bigger than you, which you cannot ram. A hit gives most of the cost back; a miss does not.

As you grow you move up tiers. At Tier 2 you crush cars, at Tier 3 you drive straight through houses and at Tier 4 the city is yours. Watch out for police helicopters that chase big, destructive tanks, and don't camp in one place: other tanks will hunt you down.

Controls

  • Desktop: WASD to drive, click to fire, hold Shift for turbo, G to throw a grenade, M to mute.
  • Phone and tablet: drag on the left of the screen to drive and tap Fire, Turbo or Grenade. The turret aims for you.

Features

  • Fast rounds against up to 20 rival tanks in a city you can destroy.
  • Supply drops, golden meteors and police helicopters keep every match different.
  • Daily, weekly and all-time leaderboards ranked by peak mass.
  • Fourteen cosmetic skins, all free or unlocked by playing.
  • Install it to your home screen to play full screen and offline.
